ABOUT AL SAHAB AL RASID

AL SAHAB AL RASID TRAD. & CONT. CO. L.L.C. (ASAR) is an established construction and engineering contracting entity registered and operating in the Sultanate of Oman. The company specializes in executing civil construction, infrastructure development, heavy concrete structures, mechanical and electrical engineering, pipeline works, and certified technical manpower supply.

Guided by an uncompromising commitment to client satisfaction, workplace safety, and engineering excellence, ASAR deploys skilled labor, experienced project engineers, modern equipment fleets, and strict QA/QC systems across both public and private sector projects in Oman.
